========================================================
  SAIKO — ONE-TIME SETUP (about 15 minutes)
  After this, you manage everything from a visual admin
  panel. No code, no folders, ever again.
========================================================

You'll connect three free things:
  GitHub  = stores your site files
  Netlify = puts your site online
  Admin   = the visual panel where you add builds

Do these in order. Take your time.

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — PUT YOUR SITE ON GITHUB
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to github.com and make a free account (or log in).

2. Click the "+" top-right > "New repository".
   - Repository name: saiko
   - Set it to "Public"
   - Do NOT tick "Add a README"
   - Click "Create repository".

3. On the next page, click "uploading an existing file"
   (a link in the middle of the page).

4. Unzip saiko-portfolio.zip on your computer. Open the
   saiko-portfolio folder. Select ALL the files and folders
   inside it (index.html, library.html, admin, content,
   images, rooms.js, etc.) and drag them onto the GitHub
   upload page.
   IMPORTANT: drag the CONTENTS of the folder, not the
   folder itself.

5. Wait for them to finish uploading, then click
   "Commit changes" (green button, bottom).

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — PUT IT ONLINE WITH NETLIFY
--------------------------------------------------------
6. Go to netlify.com and sign up — choose
   "Sign up with GitHub". This links them automatically.

7. Click "Add new site" > "Import an existing project".

8. Choose "GitHub", authorise if asked, and pick your
   "saiko" repository from the list.

9. Leave all the build settings blank/default and click
   "Deploy". Wait ~30 seconds. Your site is live at a
   random URL like random-name-123.netlify.app.

10. Rename it: Site configuration > Change site name >
    type saiko-rooms (or whatever you like) > Save.
    Your site is now saiko-rooms.netlify.app.

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — TURN ON THE ADMIN PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------
The admin panel needs a login system. Netlify has one
built in ("Identity"). Turn it on:

11. In your site's Netlify dashboard, go to:
    Integrations (or "Identity" in older menus) >
    enable "Identity".
    (If you can't find it: it may be under
    Site configuration > Identity > "Enable Identity".)

12. Under Identity > Registration, set it to
    "Invite only" (so only you can log in).

13. Still under Identity, find "Services" >
    "Git Gateway" and click "Enable Git Gateway".
    This lets the admin panel save to GitHub.

14. Go to the Identity tab > "Invite users" >
    type YOUR email > send. Check your inbox and click
    the invite link to set your password.

--------------------------------------------------------
PART 4 — YOU'RE DONE. HOW TO USE IT
--------------------------------------------------------
15. Go to  saiko-rooms.netlify.app/admin
    Log in with the email + password from step 14.

You'll see a visual panel. To add a build:
   - Click "Room builds" > "All room builds"
   - Click "Add Builds +" at the bottom of the list
   - Fill in the name, pick the type, upload a screenshot,
     write the description, tick "featured" if you want it
     on the homepage
   - Click "Save", then "Publish"

Your live site updates itself in about a minute. That's it.
No files, no code, from any device, anywhere.

To set your Discord/email: in the admin, open
"Site links" > "Contact & links" and paste them in.

--------------------------------------------------------
STUCK?
--------------------------------------------------------
The trickiest part is usually step 11-13 (Identity + Git
Gateway), because Netlify moves these menus around. If you
get lost, tell me exactly what you see on screen and I'll
point you to the right button.
